Image generation and cleanup
Character sheet mode is used by Generate missing sprites, then split into transparent PNGs for each expression. Keeps generated expressions at a consistent front-facing camera angle and crop. Green and magenta backgrounds are removed automatically. Choose a colour absent from the character. Separate images retain more detail than a large sheet; image size follows Quick Image Gen settings. Downloads a 176 MB anime cut-out model once. Runs locally on the server, around 1 GB RAM and 20 seconds per image on a small server. It can remove interior background gaps, but may also remove fine details. Export your set before redoing cleanup. Downloads a 2.5 MB anime upscaler once. Processes small sections to limit memory use and keeps transparency. Runs after cleanup; enlarging an image again makes it larger again.
