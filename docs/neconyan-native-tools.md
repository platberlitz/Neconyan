# Neconyan's included tools

Story Mode, Meower and the tools below ship with Neconyan. Open **Extensions → Manage extensions** to see their versions, authors and licences, or to turn a feature off. Their code updates with Neconyan. Custom extensions can still be installed and managed separately.

## Find the right paw for the job

- **Story Mode** turns a saved chat into a continuous manuscript. Open a chat, then choose Story Mode in Characters. Its preference is saved for that chat.
- **Meower** gives your cast a private social timeline. Open it from Characters or its chat shortcut. Timeline Settings manages the cast, personas and posts.
- **BotSearcher** helps find and import character cards from Characters. Its storage service is included. JannyAI browser support is optional and reports whether a local browser is available.
- **Dialogue Colors**, **Prompt Tags**, **Macro Enhanced**, **Deep Swipe**, and **Regex Agent Themes** keep their controls in chat, Agents or Extensions, where their work happens.
- **Preset Tools** and **Chat Completion Tabs** assist with model and prompt setup. **Prompting Lab**, **World Info Lab**, and **Lorebook Distiller** support character, prompt and lorebook work.
- **Chat Archive** and **Card & Lorebook Time Machine** keep their chat, card and lorebook recovery controls. **Debugger** is an optional diagnostic tool.
- **Termeownal UI** is optional. Enable it in Manage extensions, then use its own settings switch. Its pixel kitty follows the selected palette, and its commands open Neconyan's Home and chat modes. New settings inherit Neconyan's current palette; saved palettes remain yours. Turning it off restores the ordinary workspace.

Meower and Story Mode switch views without clearing the chat draft. Generated Neconyan cat artwork appears in their controls and empty states. Dark mode keeps its black sidebar, and light mode keeps light content surfaces.

## Saved data and compatibility

The included BotSearcher and Meower services load with Neconyan even when optional server plugins are disabled. An older installed server copy cannot replace them or load alongside them.

Imported extension folders whose names match an included feature are retained as inactive copies. The import report identifies those copies separately from custom extensions that become active after a reload. Existing enable/disable choices and legacy settings keys remain compatible.

Meower imports existing saved timelines on its first private save, leaves the original imported bytes in place, and keeps the preceding saved version. Conflicting saves are rejected so another device's changes can be recovered before retrying.
